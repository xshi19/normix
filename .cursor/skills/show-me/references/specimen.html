Math survives the trip

A research-note specimen — the GIG density, typeset by KaTeX inside this file:

$$ f(y \mid p, a, b) \;=\; \frac{(a/b)^{p/2}}{2\,K_p\!\left(\sqrt{ab}\right)}\; y^{\,p-1} \exp\!\left(-\tfrac{1}{2}\!\left(a y + \tfrac{b}{y}\right)\right), \qquad y > 0 $$

with sufficient statistics \(t(y) = (\log y,\; y,\; 1/y)\) and log-partition \(\psi(\theta) = \log 2 + \tfrac{p}{2}\log(b/a) + \log K_p(\sqrt{ab})\) — the same formulas as docs/theory/, no Sphinx build required.
